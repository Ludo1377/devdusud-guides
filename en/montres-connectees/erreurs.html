7 mistakes to avoid before buying a smartwatch

Buy a smartwatch with no regrets: the 7 most common mistakes and how to avoid them.

Most bad purchases come from the same mistakes, repeated by thousands of buyers. Here are the 7 most common — and how to avoid them easily.

Mistake #1: buying the cheapest without thinking about use

The lowest price is tempting, but a model that doesn't fit your needs will need replacing soon. Think usage first, price second.

Mistake #2: following the most promoted product

Sponsored products are not necessarily the best. Always cross-check several independent sources before deciding.

Mistake #3: ignoring recent negative reviews

1-2 star reviews from the last few months reveal manufacturing flaws and reliability issues. Don't dismiss them.

Mistake #4: forgetting consumables and accessories

Cartridges, filters, wear parts: recurring costs can double the real price. Calculate them before buying.

Mistake #5: not checking compatibility

Dimensions, plugs, apps, ecosystem: one missed detail can make the device unusable at home. Check before checkout.

Mistake #6: rushing on a flash sale

A discount is only a deal if the product already suited you at full price. Compare the real market price first.

Mistake #7: neglecting warranty and support

Without a clear warranty and reachable support, the cheapest can become the most expensive. Warranty is part of the product.
