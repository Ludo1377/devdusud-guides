en: frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions everyone asks before buying a en — no marketing fluff.

What minimum budget?

There is a 'quality floor': below 40-60 €, compromises on materials and reliability quickly cost you. Above 150 €, gains become marginal for everyday use.

New or refurbished?

Refurbished from a brand with warranty is excellent value (30-50% savings). Avoid listings without invoice or warranty.

How do I check compatibility?

Check standards and connectors BEFORE buying: an incompatible device is not always refundable. The manufacturer's site lists exact requirements.

What lifespan?

3-6 years depending on use. The battery (if built-in) and software updates age the fastest.

Does after-sales service really matter?

Yes, especially for second-tier brands: local-language support avoids endless exchanges. Read recent negative reviews to spot recurring issues.

How do I spot fake deals?

Compare the current price with its history (camelcamelcamel or similar): a 'deal' that has been at this price 3 months out of 12 is not a deal.
