an air purifier: should you go budget or premium?

Budget or premium: our honest analysis to choose an air purifier at the right price.

Should you spend 300 or 800? The truth is often in between. Here's what premium really brings, and what you can skip.

The budget option

Buying budget is no longer a bad deal: manufacturers concentrate volumes on these ranges, guaranteeing available parts and plenty of user feedback.

The premium option

Premium is justified on three points: longevity (materials and build), daily comfort (features that change everyday life) and service (extended warranties). If you use it daily, these matter.

What is the average lifespan?

Expect 5 to 10 years for a well-maintained model from a good brand. Wear parts and manufacturer warranty are the best longevity indicators.

Should I buy online or in store?

Both complement each other: try in store if possible, then compare prices online. Always check return conditions before buying.
